ABOUT FEATURED DESTINATIONS

INTRAMUROS

MANILA CITY

Known as the Walled City, it served as the political, religious, and military center of Spanish colonial rule. Today, its cobblestone streets, old fortifications, and preserved heritage buildings act as an open-air museum along the Pasig River. Foreigners should visit to see its cross-cultural history and distinctive architeture.

ATTRACTIONS

  • Fort Santiago
  • Manila Cathedral
  • Casa Manila Museum

WHITE BEACH

BORACAY, AKLAN

A 4-kilometer stretch of fine, powdery white sand and clear turquoise water on the island's west coast, divided into three distinct stations. A pedestrian-only beachfront path runs along the shore, lined with resorts, dining spots, shops, and palm trees. Foreigners should visit for its world-class sand and water, as well as its protected island charm.

ACTIVITIES

  • Paraw Sailing
  • Island Hopping
  • Water Sports

CHOCOLATE HILLS

BATUAN, BOHOL

A geological formation of roughly 1,260 to over 1,770 symmetrical, cone- and dome-shaped limestone mounds, famous for turning chocolate-brown during the dry season. Foreigners should visit for its rare geological scenery and easy day-trip pairing.

ACTIVITIES

  • Viewing Decks
  • ATV Rides
  • Ziplining

LION'S HEAD

BAGUIO CITY, BENGUET

A 40-foot (12-meter) limestone sculpture along Kennon Road in Camp 6. Unveiled in 1972 after being commissioned by the Lions Club of Baguio, it serves as a famous roadside landmark welcoming travelers to Baguio City. Foreigners should visit to see Baguio's iconic gateway and stalls selling 'pasalubong' nearby.

ACTIVITIES

  • Photo Ops
  • Costume Rentals
  • Buy Local Snacks and Souvenirs
